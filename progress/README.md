# Progress archive

Use the **Daily progress** issue form after real work on this e-commerce BI dashboard. The required fields ask what you worked on, what changed or what you learned, and evidence of the result. Keep each entry factual and do not submit filler just to add a contribution.

Useful small changes can include improving validation or error handling, adding a focused test or example, clarifying setup instructions, or documenting a result you actually observed. Pick changes that fit the current code; do not claim a metric or feature until you have verified it.

The workflow archives check-ins at `progress/YYYY-MM-DD.md` and closes the issue after archiving. It runs when a check-in is opened and once daily at 18:23 India Standard Time as a retry. With no new valid check-in, it creates no archive entry or commit.

Opening the check-in issue is your activity. The archive commit is attributed to `github-actions[bot]`, not to you. Use only the repository's public or synthetic data; do not paste customer or private data.
